About Us

Welcome to Maa Farmer Producer Sahakari Mandli, Matar — a dedicated producer cooperative committed to bringing authentic taste and quality traditional food products to every home. We work with passion and care to create products that preserve traditional flavors while maintaining high standards of quality and freshness. We specialize in a wide range of delicious products including mini papad, chakri, khakhra, fryums, snacks, and other traditional food items that are loved across generations. Our products are prepared with carefully selected ingredients and a focus on taste, hygiene, and consistency. At Maa Farmer Producer Sahakari Mandli, Matar, we believe in supporting local producers and promoting traditional food culture while delivering products that families can trust and enjoy. Our mission is to provide wholesome, flavorful, and high-quality products that bring people together through the joy of food. We are committed to preserving tradition, ensuring quality, and serving our customers with dedication and excellence.
